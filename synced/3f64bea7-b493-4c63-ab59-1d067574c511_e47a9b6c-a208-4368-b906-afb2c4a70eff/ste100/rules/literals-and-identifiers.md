# Literals and identifiers

Treat quoted display text, placards, labels, switch positions, part numbers, drawing references, formulas, code, filenames, product names, standards titles, and legally mandated wording as literals. Preserve spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and spacing when exactness is required. Exclude literals from ordinary vocabulary corrections, but audit their boundaries and explain any conflict with STE. Do not confuse an identifier with a general noun when applying articles.
