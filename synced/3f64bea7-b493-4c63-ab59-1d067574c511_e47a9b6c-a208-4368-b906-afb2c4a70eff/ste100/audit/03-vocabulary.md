# Vocabulary

For every non-literal word, check dictionary approval, approved meaning (including any restricted-context meaning), part of speech, and dictionary-listed form. Check technical nouns/verbs against the subject-field glossary and category, including that a technical noun stays a noun and a technical verb stays a verb (rules 1.7, 1.13). Flag slang, regional terms, jargon, noun/verb conversion, unauthorized synonyms for the same item, and inconsistent spelling. Flag a phrasal verb built from two individually approved words (rule 9.3) even where the dictionary does not itself mark it non-approved. Check pronoun use: only dictionary-approved pronouns with an unambiguous single antecedent (see GR-3, GR-4 in [rules/general-recommendations.md](../rules/general-recommendations.md)); gendered pronouns and "man"/"woman" outside a genuine contextual need are a `MAJOR` finding under GR-7. Use `UNVERIFIED` when the authority was not supplied, per [audit/00-severity.md](00-severity.md) — never guess an approval.
