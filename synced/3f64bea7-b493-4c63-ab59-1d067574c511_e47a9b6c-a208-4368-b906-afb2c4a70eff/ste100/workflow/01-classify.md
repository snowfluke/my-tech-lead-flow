# 01 — Classify

Before editing, identify the document purpose, reader, safety criticality, and governing publication specification. Mark each block:

- `PROCEDURE`: a reader must perform an action.
- `DESCRIPTION`: the document explains a system, condition, function, or result.
- `SAFETY`: a warning, caution, danger, prohibition, or injury/damage control.
- `NOTE`: supplementary information that is not required to perform the task.
- `LITERAL`: quoted display text, label, identifier, code, formula, title, or mandated wording.

Split mixed paragraphs. A note that contains a command, limit, result, or safety control is misclassified and must be moved. Record classification boundaries before drafting.
