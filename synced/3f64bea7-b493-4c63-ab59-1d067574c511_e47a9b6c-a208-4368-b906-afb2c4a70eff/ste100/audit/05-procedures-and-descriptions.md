# Procedures and descriptions

For procedures, check imperative commands, one instruction per sentence (unless simultaneous or an inseparable immediate result), prerequisite-condition-comma-command order, numbered sequence, and the 20-word limit. Check every note: information only, never an instruction/limit/imperative, 25-word sentence cap, and that the procedure still works if all notes were deleted.

For descriptions, check one topic per sentence with concrete values (not vague generalities), the 25-word sentence limit, key-word/connecting-word continuity between sentences (no synonym-swapping for the same referent), explicit cause/condition/sequence relationships, no hidden commands, one topic per paragraph with a topic sentence, a six-sentence-per-paragraph maximum, and correct use of vertical lists (no mixed procedural/descriptive items in one list; a repeated `DO NOT` per item, not stated once above the list).
