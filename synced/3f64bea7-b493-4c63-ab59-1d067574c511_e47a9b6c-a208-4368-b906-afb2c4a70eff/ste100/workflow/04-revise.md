# 04 — Revise

Run separate passes and stop if a pass changes technical meaning:

1. Source fidelity: compare every requirement, number, condition, result, exception, and safety control.
2. Classification: ensure commands, descriptions, notes, and safety text are in the correct form.
3. Terminology: enforce one canonical term per item and check dictionary/glossary approval.
4. Grammar: check part of speech, approved verb forms, active voice, articles, complete clauses, and forbidden contractions.
5. Structure: split long sentences, use vertical lists, sequence steps, and keep conditions before commands.
6. Safety and mechanics: check signal words, negative commands, literals, punctuation, units, and word counts.

Re-run the audit after every meaning-affecting correction. A clean mechanical scan is not evidence of dictionary compliance.
