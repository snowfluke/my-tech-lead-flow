# Grammar and sentences

Check complete clauses, active voice, allowed tenses (infinitive, imperative, simple present/past/future, past participle as adjective only), auxiliary constructions, progressive/`-ing` misuse, nominalizations, contractions, and one clear topic/action per sentence (concrete, not vague, in descriptive text). Confirm that a passive sentence in descriptive text has a genuinely unknown agent and that changing it would alter meaning.

Check article and demonstrative use per rule 4.5: an article or demonstrative should be present before an applicable noun unless the noun refers to a general/unspecified category, and a definite article must not precede a noun immediately followed by an alphanumeric identifier (for example, "circuit breaker 36L7," not "the circuit breaker 36L7"). Check the advisory General Recommendations where relevant — unambiguous "that," "with," and "this," no false friends or Latin abbreviations, inclusive language (no gendered pronouns or "man"/"woman" absent a genuine contextual need), and cautious use of the possessive `'s` — see [rules/general-recommendations.md](../rules/general-recommendations.md); treat a GR deviation as advisory unless it also creates a genuine ambiguity a numbered rule covers.
