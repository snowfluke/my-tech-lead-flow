# Punctuation and word count

Count every procedural sentence, note, and warning/caution sentence against the applicable limit (20 words procedural/safety, 25 words descriptive and notes). Flag any semicolon as a `MAJOR` finding — STE bans it outright; the fix is always to split into two sentences. Check comma placement after initial conditions, colons before vertical lists (the colon closes the lead-in sentence like a period; each item after it is its own sentence under the same cap), list-item capitalization and periods, hyphenation of unit-noun phrases, and that parentheses are used only for one of STE's seven approved purposes (illustration/text reference, item ID, work-step number, abbreviation, singular/plural marker, short explanatory aside, or alternative). Apply STE's own word-counting conventions — see [rules/08-punctuation-and-word-count.md](../rules/08-punctuation-and-word-count.md) — rather than deferring counts to the publication specification; the publication specification governs formatting, not STE word counts.
