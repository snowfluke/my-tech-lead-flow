# 03 — Draft

Draft in this order: terminology and literals, content meaning, block classification, sentence structure, then word-level STE checks. Use active voice and complete sentences. Use imperative commands for procedures. Put conditions first, followed by a comma and the command. Use vertical lists for complex sequences or sets of items; do not mix procedure and description in one list.

Do not paraphrase a requirement into weaker language. Do not use a “simpler” synonym until its approved meaning and part of speech are checked. Keep unresolved vocabulary visible as `UNVERIFIED` in the audit record, not in the published document.
