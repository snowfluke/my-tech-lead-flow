# Safety instructions

Confirm the signal word or symbol matches the actual assessed risk: WARNING for injury/death risk, CAUTION for damage-only risk, and WARNING when both are present — treat an under-classified risk (a real injury/death hazard marked CAUTION) as a `BLOCKER`, not a style note. A project may use a different signal vocabulary or graphic (danger, attention, notice, ISO/ANSI symbols) as long as the content still satisfies the checks below.

Check that each safety instruction leads with a direct command or condition-first structure, uses explicit `DO NOT` for prohibitions, is placed before the reader is exposed to the hazard, and states the concrete consequence of not obeying it wherever possible. Check preservation of exact required signal words and literal safety text, and the 20-word sentence limit from Section 5. Safety information left in a note is a `BLOCKER` until moved into the procedure or a correctly classified WARNING/CAUTION.
