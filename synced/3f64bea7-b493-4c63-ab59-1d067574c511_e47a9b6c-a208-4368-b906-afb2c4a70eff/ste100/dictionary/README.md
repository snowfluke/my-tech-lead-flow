# External dictionary boundary

This directory intentionally contains no ASD-STE100 dictionary entries. ASD-STE100 and its Part 2 dictionary are copyrighted ASD material, and the standard states that receiving or possessing a copy does not automatically grant a license to reproduce or publish it.

Before a vocabulary audit, the user or organization must provide a lawful local source or an authorized checker. Confirm the applicable special usage right or written permission with the organization's legal or compliance owner. Do not upload the source to a public repository, paste bulk entries into prompts, extract it into JSON, or commit generated indexes.

The skill may use:

- a local, lawfully accessible copy supplied at audit time;
- an organization-approved checker that accepts a word, part of speech, and context;
- the organization-owned glossary in `company-terms.yaml`;
- the source document's exact literals and identifiers.

This guidance is operational, not legal advice. “ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English” is used only to identify the referenced standard. This skill does not claim ASD endorsement, certification, or affiliation.
